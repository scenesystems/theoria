# Theoria homepage — what to look at, and how to tell me what you saw

Preview: https://theoria-pr-93.staging.scenesystems.io

Thanks for doing this. You do not need to understand what the site is about. I want your eyes on how it looks, how it feels to use, and anything that seems broken, cramped, jumpy, or confusing — especially on the phone, tablet, or computer you actually own, because I can only test on mine.

Budget about 20–30 minutes. Do the whole walk-through once on each device you have. If you only have time for one device, use your phone.

---

## 1. Before you start: tell me your setup

For each device, send me this line first (copy and fill in):

> Device: ______ (e.g. iPhone 14, Samsung S23, iPad Air, MacBook Air 13", Windows laptop, 27" monitor)
> Browser: ______ (Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Samsung Internet)
> Light or dark mode on the device: ______
> Anything unusual: large text setting on, "reduce motion" on, browser zoomed, etc.

How to take a screenshot:

- iPhone: side button + volume up
- Android: power + volume down
- Mac: Cmd + Shift + 4, then drag
- Windows: Win + Shift + S
- Screen recording (for anything that moves or flickers): iPhone Control Center → record; Android Quick Settings → Screen record; Mac: Cmd + Shift + 5; Windows: Win + G

Screenshots are the most useful thing you can send me. A screenshot plus one sentence beats a paragraph.

## 2. How to give feedback

Please describe things with these four pieces:

1. **Where** — which part of the page (use the names in this document, e.g. "the Brief box", "the paper", "the discs").
2. **What you saw** — plain words: "text cut off on the right", "button jumped when I tapped", "too much empty space", "I couldn't tell this was clickable".
3. **What you expected** instead.
4. **Screenshot** or short recording.

Everything counts, including:

- "Ugly", "cramped", "too much empty space", "too pale to read" — taste reactions are wanted.
- "I didn't know I could tap that."
- "I didn't understand what this was for."
- Anything that moved, flashed, or jumped when you didn't expect it.
- Anything that took longer than a second or two.
- Anything where you had to scroll sideways.

Don't worry about whether it's "really a bug". If it felt off, tell me.

---

## 3. The page, top to bottom (what each part is called)

The page has four parts, in order:

1. **Header** — a thin bar at the very top: the Theoria name on the left; on the right, a "Docs" link, a GitHub icon, and a light/dark toggle.
2. **Hero** — a large headline "Scientific computing and model programming with Effect", a short paragraph, a filled button "Browse the packages", and a plain text link "See how it's built".
3. **The demonstration** — titled "The packages at work". This is the big interactive piece, described in section 5. It contains:
   - a **Scenario** chooser with three options: **Unfinished light**, **Lost market**, **Drowned library**
   - a **Brief** text box holding a short description of an imaginary place
   - the **paper** — a pale rectangle with a short piece of writing on it and a handful of small circles (I'll call them **discs**), each with a name written near it
   - a set of **proposals** (short suggestions, each with a **Merge** switch)
   - a strip of small dots/knots and version codes beside the paper (**version strand**)
   - a "**How it's built**" section with three tabs — **Compose**, **Arrange**, **Build** — each showing a block of code
4. **Footer** — the bottom of the page.

On a laptop or desktop, the paper is pinned on the right and stays in view while the rest scrolls on the left. On a phone or tablet, everything stacks vertically.

## 4. First impressions (do this before touching anything)

Open the page fresh and just look. Then answer:

- [ ] Did anything flash, flicker, or visibly rearrange itself in the first two seconds? (A brief grey placeholder before the paper appears is expected; a jump, a red error, or a blank box is not.)
- [ ] On first look, without scrolling, what do you see? Take a screenshot of exactly what's on screen when the page opens. **This one screenshot is very important to me on every device.**
- [ ] Does the big headline wrap onto a sensible number of lines? (On a phone, 3–4 lines is fine. If a single word is stranded on its own line, or it's more than 4 lines, tell me.)
- [ ] Is the text readable — size, contrast against the background?
- [ ] Does the page feel like one continuous surface, or like a pile of boxes on boxes? (I'm aiming for continuous.)
- [ ] Does anything look cut off at the left or right edge?
- [ ] Try swiping or scrolling sideways. **The page should never scroll sideways.** If it does, screenshot it and tell me where.

Now scroll slowly all the way to the bottom, then all the way back up:

- [ ] Is the spacing between sections comfortable — nothing feels crammed, nothing feels like a huge empty gap?
- [ ] On a laptop/desktop: does the paper on the right stay put while the left side scrolls? Does it ever overlap or cover text?
- [ ] On a phone: when the paper scrolls off the top of the screen, a thin strip with the small discs should appear pinned near the top (the "band"). Does it appear? Does it cover anything you were reading or trying to tap? Does tapping it take you back to the paper?
- [ ] When you scroll back up, does the band disappear cleanly?

## 5. The demonstration — try each interaction

### 5a. The header

- [ ] Tap/click the light/dark toggle. Does the whole page change smoothly? Anything left the wrong color (a bright white box in dark mode, or a dark patch in light mode)? Screenshot both modes.
- [ ] Do the header items look evenly spaced and aligned? On a phone, do they all fit on one line without crowding?
- [ ] Does "Docs" go to a docs page? Does the back button return you to the homepage in the same place you left?

### 5b. The hero

- [ ] Hover (laptop) or tap (phone) "Browse the packages". Does it look and feel like a button? Does it go somewhere?
- [ ] Click/tap "See how it's built". It should scroll you smoothly down to the demonstration. Did it land somewhere sensible, or did it overshoot / hide the heading under the top of the screen?

### 5c. Scenario chooser

- [ ] Are all three options visible and readable? On a phone, do they fit — or do they run off the edge?
- [ ] Choose a different scenario. The Brief text and the paper should change. Does the paper's height jolt violently, or does it settle smoothly?
- [ ] Do the discs move to new places, or does the drawing just snap to a new picture? (Some movement is intended. Tell me whether it felt calm or frantic.)
- [ ] Does the writing on the paper swap cleanly, or do you see two versions overlapping for a moment?
- [ ] Switch scenarios quickly several times in a row. Does anything get stuck, show the wrong writing under the wrong title, or leave a little empty pop-up behind?
- [ ] Is it clear which scenario is currently selected?

### 5d. The Brief box

- [ ] Tap into it and type or delete a few words. On a phone, does the keyboard cover the box? Does the page jump when the keyboard opens?
- [ ] While the place rebuilds, does anything under the box shove downward, then back up? (A little is tolerable; a big lurch is not.)
- [ ] Delete everything. Is the message that appears clear and calm, not alarming?
- [ ] Is there a word/character count or status text near it? Is it readable?

### 5e. The paper and the discs

- [ ] Do the disc names overlap each other or overlap the writing? Screenshot any collision.
- [ ] Do any discs sit half off the edge of the paper?
- [ ] **Phone:** tap a disc. A small explanation should open. Was the disc easy to hit with your thumb, or did you have to try more than once? Did you accidentally hit a neighbouring disc or a line of text instead? **Tell me honestly — I suspect the discs are too small.**
- [ ] **Laptop:** hover over a disc without clicking. After a brief pause an explanation should appear. Move away — it should close after a moment. Did it flicker on/off as you moved between nearby discs?
- [ ] Hover or tap a **line of the writing** on the paper. It should also open an explanation (this one waits slightly longer before opening). Does the line highlight so you know what you're pointing at?
- [ ] Open an explanation, then tap/click somewhere blank. Does it close?
- [ ] Does the explanation pop-up ever get cut off by the edge of the screen, or appear somewhere far from what you touched?
- [ ] Is the text inside the pop-up readable? Are its edges/shadow tasteful or heavy?

### 5f. Proposals and the Merge switch

- [ ] Are the proposals easy to tell apart from one another? Are they readable? Do they look like a list, or like a stack of boxes?
- [ ] Hover or tap a proposal's name. Does a disc on the paper (and maybe a line of writing) light up in response?
- [ ] Flip a **Merge** switch on. Watch the paper: the writing should change and a new disc should arrive. Did the arrival look intentional (a dashed ring, then a solid disc) or glitchy? Did the paper's height jump?
- [ ] After merging, the proposal should show "Merged" and a new entry should appear in the version strand beside the paper. Did it?
- [ ] Flip it back off. Does everything return cleanly?
- [ ] Flip two switches quickly. Does anything get confused?
- [ ] **Phone:** when you flip a switch, the paper may be above you, off screen. Could you tell what changed? Did the band at the top show you anything useful? (I want to know if this cause-and-effect is lost on a phone.)

### 5g. Version strand, signature, and the long code

- [ ] Beside the paper is a column of small dots/knots and long codes. Do they align with the paper? Do the long codes wrap neatly, or spill out?
- [ ] Hover/tap a knot or a code. An explanation should open. On one of the long codes there's a **Copy** control. Tap it — it should say "Copied" and the explanation should stay open, not vanish.
- [ ] Anything here look like a "badge" or "pill"? I'm trying to avoid those — tell me if anything reads as a sticker.

### 5h. "How it's built" — the code tabs

- [ ] Are the three tabs (Compose, Arrange, Build) clearly tabs? Is the current one obvious (a thin underline)?
- [ ] Tap each tab. Does the code area change without the page lurching?
- [ ] On a phone, does the code block scroll sideways _inside itself_ (fine) or does it push the whole page sideways (not fine)?
- [ ] Is the code readable in both light and dark mode — colors not muddy, not neon?
- [ ] **Laptop:** hover a line number in the grey gutter on the left. Some line numbers open an explanation. Did the cursor or the line change to hint that it was interactive?
- [ ] Some code lines have small notes attached (annotations). On a phone, tap one. Does the note open? Is it legible?
- [ ] Below the code, "In the reference" and "Source" list links. Do they line up? Do they work?

### 5i. Footer

- [ ] Does it look finished and intentional, or like an afterthought?
- [ ] Everything readable and aligned? Links work?

## 6. Device-specific extra checks

### Phone (any)

- [ ] Turn the phone sideways (landscape). Does the page reflow sensibly? Anything cut off?
- [ ] In Settings, turn on your phone's **larger text** / bold text setting and reload. Does anything overlap or get cut off?
- [ ] Pinch-zoom on the paper. Does it zoom like a normal page?
- [ ] Do a fast "flick" scroll down the whole page and back. Does the band at the top flicker, lag, or get stuck?
- [ ] Does the page feel sluggish while a scenario is rebuilding — does scrolling stutter?
- [ ] Battery/heat: if your phone gets warm sitting on this page for a minute, tell me.

### iPhone specifically (Safari)

- [ ] With the address bar at the bottom, does the band or the footer get hidden behind Safari's bar?
- [ ] Rotate to landscape and back. Anything broken after rotating?

### Android specifically (Chrome or Samsung Internet)

- [ ] Any difference between Chrome and Samsung Internet if you have both?
- [ ] Does the system dark mode carry into the page automatically?

### Tablet (iPad / Android tablet)

- [ ] Portrait: does it look like a big phone (stacked) or a small laptop (side by side)? Screenshot it — I want to see which it chose at your size.
- [ ] Landscape: same question.
- [ ] Try it with a keyboard attached if you have one: press Tab repeatedly. Can you see where the focus is at all times? Does the band at the top ever cover the thing that's focused?
- [ ] Split-screen with another app, if your tablet allows it. Does the page cope with the narrow width?

### Laptop / desktop

- [ ] Take a screenshot of the first screen without scrolling. I especially want to know: **can you see the top of the paper and at least one disc without scrolling?**
- [ ] Make the browser window narrower by dragging its edge — go from full width down to as narrow as it will go, slowly. Watch for the moment the layout switches from side-by-side to stacked. Does anything break, overlap, or jump at that moment? At any width, does a horizontal scrollbar appear?
- [ ] Zoom the browser to 150% and 200% (Cmd/Ctrl and +). Reload. Any horizontal scrollbar? Does the headline stay to 3 lines or fewer? Is anything hidden?
- [ ] **Keyboard only, no mouse:** click once in the address bar, then press Tab repeatedly to move through the page. You should always be able to see a visible outline showing where you are. Press Enter or Space on a disc — an explanation should open. Press Escape — it should close and put you back where you were. On the code tabs, arrow keys should move between tabs and Enter should switch. Does anything trap you, or become invisible?
- [ ] Hover over discs and proposal names and move the mouse around quickly. Does the pop-up flicker, lag, or open the wrong one?
- [ ] Does the pinned paper stay properly aligned as you scroll, or drift?
- [ ] If you have Firefox as well as Chrome/Safari/Edge, try both and tell me if anything differs.

### Large monitor (27" or bigger, or ultrawide)

- [ ] Does the page sit comfortably in the middle, or does it stretch too wide / look lost in empty space?
- [ ] Is the headline size reasonable, or comically large?

### Accessibility settings (if you're willing)

- [ ] Turn on **Reduce Motion** (iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Motion; Mac: System Settings → Accessibility → Display; Windows: Settings → Accessibility → Visual effects → Animation effects off; Android: Settings → Accessibility → Remove animations). Reload. Switch scenarios and flip a Merge switch. Things should now change with gentle fades rather than sliding around. Tell me anything that still slides. (I already know the Merge switch's little knob still slides — anything else?)
- [ ] Windows: turn on a **High Contrast** theme (Settings → Accessibility → Contrast themes). Reload. Can you still see the discs, the tab underline, the switches, and the focus outline when you Tab? Screenshot it.
